# Round 14: click-14, by mouse

The diary of one of the blind playtesters: a fresh AI agent that knew nothing of the game, played it through a text front end and wrote down what it did and what got in its way. How the rounds worked, and what each one changed, is told on [Playing it](playing-it.md); this round is in [The third milestone](playing-it.md#the-third-milestone-every-way-of-playing-wins-blind), and every tester's numbers are in [How far each tester got](playing-it.md#how-far-each-tester-got).

- **Round:** 14, 18 September 2026
- **Mode:** click: numbered options only, as a mouse player
- **Start:** from the start (Deck Nine, turn 0)
- **Result:** Did not win. Where play ended: Lawanda Platform, turn 247, with 32 points.
- **Commands:** 260, with no deaths

**About the numbers.** Its goal was Lawanda Platform, and it stopped there on arrival. The diary and README say about 275 commands; the transcript has 260.

The diary below is `playtests/2026-09-18/2026-09-18-click-14.md`, copied by `scripts/wiki-diaries.mjs`. The tester's words are unchanged; only its layout is adjusted where the wiki would draw it badly, and its headings sit one level down.

## Playtest diary: click-14

This is round 14 (click-only, from the start).

### Entry 1 (before command 1)
Starting a new click-only session. I plan to read the opening, look at what options the screen offers, and explore.

What happened (commands 1-8): the game opened on Deck Nine with a clean option list. I waited; Blather swaggered in, gave me demerits, and left. The options stayed tidy and the numbering is stable. Nothing has blown up yet, so I'm waiting more.

### Entry 2 (before command 9)
Still on Deck Nine at turn 7. Plan: keep waiting for something to happen, and head for the escape pod when it does.

What happened (commands 9-16): the alien ambassador handed me a brochure, then the explosion hit and the pod door opened. "go west / in" took me into the pod (+3), and "get in" on the safety web strapped me in. The Feinstein blew up and the pod is now heading for an ocean planet.

### Entry 3 (before command 17)
In the pod's safety web, turn 16. Plan: wait for the landing, then climb out and go up.

What happened (commands 17-33): lots of waiting while the pod crossed space and landed near an island. A panel opened with a survival kit and a towel. Once I was out of the web, "take" buttons for both showed up (a helpful "get out of the safety web (to reach what is on the floor)" button hinted at why). The pod started sinking; I grabbed both, opened the bulkhead and went up into the water. Now Underwater, "depth 3 of 3" in the status line.

### Entry 4 (before command 34)
Underwater, turn 31. The exits include "go up" plain and several "Underwater" ones. Plan: keep going up to reach the surface and land.

What happened (commands 34-39): three "go up" presses took me from depth 3 to the surface, with a new line of flavour text each time, and a fourth landed me on the Crag (+3, score 6). Then up to the Balcony and the Winding Stair.

### Entry 5 (before command 40)
Winding Stair, turn 37, score 6. Plan: climb to the top and explore the complex above, looking for food, water and anything useful.

What happened (commands 40-43): up into a ruined castle Courtyard, north to a Plain Hall, north to a Rec Area. There's a locked door north with a combination dial set to 0 ("set to a number <value>"). I tried going north anyway; "The door is closed" cost no turn.

### Entry 6 (before command 44)
Rec Area, turn 40. Plan: look around east and back through the halls for a combination, food and water; read the brochure along the way.

What happened (commands 44-49): east to Rec Corridor, north through Dorm B to SanFac B (dead end), back and south into Dorm A. Visited exits now show their destination name ("go south, Rec Corridor"), which is a handy breadcrumb.

### Entry 7 (before command 50)
Dorm A, turn 46. Plan: skip the SanFac, head back north and east along Rec Corridor to find the rest of the complex.

What happened (commands 50-56): east along to the Mess Corridor (padlocked door north) and the Dorm Corridor (a long dead walkway east). Back and south into the Mess Hall, where I took an octagonal canteen. There's a closed door south with a slot beside it. I opened the brochure's "..." menu: examine, drop, read.

### Entry 8 (before command 57)
Mess Hall, turn 52. Plan: read the brochure, open the survival kit and canteen to see what I have, then try the south door.

What happened (commands 57-62): the brochure is a lovely joke. The survival kit holds red, brown and green goo, each with an "eat" button. I guessed 108 was the canteen and got a clear "not on screen now" message (it is 113). The ID card offers "slide through slot"; the slot rejected it ("Inkorekt awtharazaashun kard"). The canteen opened but it's empty.

### Entry 9 (before command 63)
Mess Hall, turn 56. I need a different card for the kitchen door. Plan: head east down the long Dorm Corridor to explore the rest of the complex.

What happened (commands 63-68): peeked into Dorm D (empty), then east along the dead walkway: one click took me to Corridor Junction with "(That walk took about 3 hours.)". One more east is the Elevator Lobby: a blue door north and a red door south with call buttons, and a booth to the east.

### Entry 10 (before command 69)
Elevator Lobby, turn 62. Plan: push the elevator buttons to see what happens, then explore north and south from Corridor Junction.

What happened (commands 69-74): both call buttons worked; while the blue one was on its way a "wait for the elevator" button appeared, which is thoughtful. Both doors opened. The Upper Elevator (north) has Up/Down buttons and a slot, so I will need a card for it too.

### Entry 11 (before command 75)
Upper Elevator, turn 68. Plan: leave, go back to Corridor Junction and explore north and south for cards and tools.

What happened (commands 75-82): north from Corridor Junction is Admin Corridor South. Examining its crevice revealed a shiny steel key, which I can't reach with my fingers. East is SanFac E, where a hunger warning appeared with a ready-made "eat the blob of red goo" button and a time estimate on the diagnose button. The goo was cherry pie and quenched thirst too.

### Entry 12 (before command 83)
Admin Corridor South, turn 76. I need something thin or magnetic to fish out the key. Plan: explore north along the Admin corridor.

What happened (commands 83-88): north of Admin Corridor South the building is torn open by a rift eight meters wide; west of it, Systems Monitors shows several systems malfunctioning (defence, course control, communications, project control). I came back south past the crevice and down into Mech Corridor North, with rooms east and west.

### Entry 13 (before command 89)
Mech Corridor North, turn 82. Plan: check the east and west rooms here and further south for something to fish the key out with, and a way across the rift.

What happened (commands 89-94): Storage East had an oil can and a box of parts. Taking the oil can made me drop both it and the ID card ("too much for you to carry"); I didn't notice until after I'd taken the good bedistor and left. West of Mech Corridor North is a big, dim Physical Plant.

### Entry 14 (before command 95)
Physical Plant, turn 86. I'm overloaded: the brush and brochure are dead weight. Plan: drop them, go southeast and keep exploring the Mech area.

What happened (commands 95-101): dropped the brush and brochure through their "..." menus (two steps each). Southeast to Mech Corridor, east into Reactor Control (a closed reactor elevator door with a button, and a dark stairway down, which I'm avoiding without a light). Then south to Mech Corridor South, with doors southwest, south and southeast.

### Entry 15 (before command 102)
Mech Corridor South, turn 92. Plan: check all three doors here.

What happened (commands 102-107): southwest is the Tool Room, with a flask, a curved metal bar with filings on its ends (a magnet, I bet), pliers, and a laser with an old battery. I took the bar and the laser. East of it is the Machine Shop, with a chemical dispenser and nine coloured buttons (coolants, catalysts, base, acid). Its buttons are listed one per line; easy to read.

### Entry 16 (before command 108)
Machine Shop, turn 98. Plan: check east of here, then take the magnet back to the crevice for the key.

What happened (commands 108-113): the Robot Shop had one nearly working robot. "turn on" gave +2 (score 8), and after a wait he woke up: Floyd! He wants to play Hider-and-Seeker and complains something is stuck in his compartment. His "..." menu offers examine, talk to, salute, attack, listen to, search.

### Entry 17 (before command 114)
Robot Shop, turn 102, with Floyd. Plan: search Floyd for whatever is itching him, then take the magnet to the crevice.

What happened (commands 114-119): searching Floyd tickled him and a lower elevator access card fell out. My load was too heavy to take it; the game told me exactly what to drop ("Dropping the good ninety-ohm bedistor would make enough room"), so I put the bedistor in my uniform instead and took the card (+1, score 9). Then a warning: the curved bar is a magnet, and carrying it with the card will smear the stripe.

### Entry 18 (before command 120)
Robot Shop, turn 105. Plan: drop the card here right away, take the magnet up to the crevice for the key, then come back for the card.

What happened (commands 120-127): dropped the card in the Robot Shop and walked back north with Floyd following. At the crevice, the key now offers "take with magnet". (The bar's own menu only has examine and drop, so the action lives on the key, not the tool.) It's night now.

### Entry 19 (before command 128)
Admin Corridor South, turn 111. Plan: take the key with the magnet, try it on the padlock in the Mess Corridor, and fetch the card on the way back.

What happened (commands 128-134): the magnet pulled the steel key out of the crevice. I dropped the magnet there, walked back west (another "3 hours" walk) to the Mess Corridor, and "unlock with key" on the padlock sprang it open.

Mess Corridor, turn 117. Plan: take the padlock off, open the north door and see what's behind it.

What happened (commands 135-138): took the padlock, opened the door and went into Storage West: a tin can and a ladder. Score jumped to 13. The ladder is too heavy unless I drop the laser, the survival kit, the towel and the padlock, all of which the game listed for me.

Storage West, turn 120. Plan: drop those four here (I can come back for the goo), take the ladder, and carry it to the rift in the Admin Corridor.

What happened (commands 139-150): dropped the laser, kit, towel and padlock, took the ladder, and carried it back to the rift (Floyd bumped into me at the junction, cute). At the rift, nothing offers to lay the ladder across. The ladder's menu is examine, drop, open.

### Report
- **How far:** reached Lawanda Platform, the stopping point, on turn 247 (morning of the second day, Septem 7) with a score of 32.
- **Commands:** about 275 calls to the game, counting a handful of option reprints and one status check; 247 game turns.
- **Deaths:** none. I collapsed asleep on the Storage West floor once (harmless) and crashed the shuttle into the Lawanda end wall (I survived).
- **Biggest blockers:**
  1. The shuttle's night lockout. Nothing told me before I rode down that the shuttle keeps hours, so I had to make a long round trip for food and sleep (rows 24, 26).
  2. Survival timers. The diagnose "hours" didn't map to turns in any way I could predict (row 23), and eating goo from a kit on the floor took three turns because the kit had to come into my hands first and hit the weight limit (row 26).
  3. The shuttle arrival. At display 5 with the lever centred, I smashed into the wall. The "15 / 10 / 5" red-light signs were the cue, but it wasn't clear they meant "be at this speed", or that 5 is still too fast (row 28).
  4. Inventory weight. A card dropped silently on two pickups; I only noticed afterwards (row 13).
- **Best moments:** waking Floyd and searching him for the itchy card; "take with magnet" showing up on the key; the ladder's open-then-"put across rift" chain; context buttons like "wait for the elevator", "eat the blob of red goo (starving!)" and "sleep now!"; and a diagnose button that reads out hunger and sleep countdowns. The Blow'k-bibben-Gordo brochure joke landed too.

### Verdict
Yes. The point-and-click interface let me play the whole opening, from Deck Nine to Lawanda Platform, without ever typing a verb: every puzzle I met (magnet, padlock, ladder, cards, dispenser, shuttle lever) showed up as a clear button once I had the right thing. The weak spots were planning ones, not interface ones: the timers, the weight limit and the shuttle's hours and arrival speed. Those need clearer up-front signals when a clicker can't just experiment freely.
