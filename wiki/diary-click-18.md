## Playtest diary: click-18

Round 18: click-only, from the `lawanda` checkpoint (turn 260, score 42, day 2), played as a patient adult who played the original Planetfall once in 1983 and remembers it only vaguely. Goal: the labs, the cryo area, the endgame.

Result: **won.** Score 80 of 80, "Galactic Overlord", at turn 431 (171 turns from the checkpoint), day 2, dusk.

### Entries

#### Turn 260, Lawanda Platform
The briefing was good: I knew what I had, where I'd been, and that I feel "weak and flushed". I remember there was a disease, a computer and a sad scene with Floyd, and not much else. The option list offers a "stairway" (examine, climb up) as well as the escalator as scenery. The room text only mentions an escalator, so I wasn't sure whether they were the same thing. Examining the escalator: "I see nothing special about the escalator." Floyd bounded in a turn later. Went up (23).

#### Turn 263, Escalator / Fork / Systems Corridor West
Escalator, then Fork (NE/SE), then NE to Systems Corridor West. Doorway NW, stairs down north, corridor east.

#### Turn 266, Infirmary
A red spool, a medicine bottle ("Dizeez supreshun medisin -- eksperimentul") and a sealed emergency ration. Take all went badly. "The shuttle access card slips from your arms while taking the red spool... too much for you to carry", with a helpful hint that my uniform has a pocket. The ration also didn't fit, and the game suggested dropping the bedistor. I liked those hints. The pocket held only the two cards.

Opened the bottle. The bottle's "…" menu (136) says examine, drop, open, look inside, read, search. The medicine's "…" menu (137) says only "examine, drop". I had to reprint the whole list to find "140 drink" sitting outside the menu. Drank it: "the fever breaks, and you feel your strength returning." Diagnose: perfect health. I then took the spool and the ration.

#### Turn 280, Systems Corridor, Planetary Defense
Planetary Defense has a blinking light, "Surkit Boord Faalyur", and a closed access panel. Examining the light: "I see nothing special about the light." That's odd when the room text just described it. Opened the panel and found four fromitz boards. I examined all four and got the exact same text each time. Tried taking the first: "You jerk your hand back as you receive a powerful shock". No pliers or insulated option. Parked it.

#### Turn 292, Course Control
"Bedistur Faalyur!" Opened the metal cube and found a fused bedistor. Take: "fused to its socket". Only after that failure did a new entry, "take with pliers", appear under the bedistor. The pliers' own "…" menu only ever said examine/drop, so the only clue was the retry. It worked, and I put the good bedistor in: +6 (48). The label reads "take with pliers (fused ninety-ohm bedistor)", which is clumsy but clear enough.

#### Turn 302, Library Lobby
"You catch a glimpse of a small object nestled among the dust", but the options already say "green spool: examine · take · read", so the mystery is spoiled. There's a "librarian" scenery item. I clicked "47 examine" on it and got "You call for the librarian. Your voice goes flat against the shelves..." A nice line, but the button says examine and the game treats it as calling. Taking the spool made the ration fall (weight again, and my fever was back by turn 309). I dropped the empty canteen.

After I dropped the ration, the room said: "A sealed emergency ration, stamped with a red cross, lies on the counter." There's no counter in the Library Lobby. That's the Infirmary's description following the ration around.

Terminal: "40 type a number 5" (Xe Prajekt), then 1/2/3. "Type a number" as a label is clunky ("type 5" would do). The disease text and the "call the librarian" hint were both consistent.

#### Turn 309, Booth 3
A teleport booth with a slot and buttons 1/2. I noted it but never ended up using it.

#### Turn 317, Library
Put the red spool in the microfilm reader and turned it on. It says death comes 8-10 days after the first symptoms. Good, I have time.

#### Turn 323, Main Lab, Lab Storage
Main Lab has a radiation-lock NE, a bio-lock SE, a computer room SW and a storage room S. In the storage room I searched the lab uniform: teleportation card and a paper, "Kombinaashun tuu Konfurins Ruum: 982". There was also a fresh laser battery. I swapped it into the laser (117 drop on the old one worked straight out of the laser, which was nice).

#### Turn 335, Computer Room / Miniaturization Booth / ProjCon Office
Floyd: "Uh oh. Computer is broken." The printout says "Malfunkshun in Sekshun 384". The booth to the south wants a card I don't have. ProjCon Office has a mural that "seems to ripple now and then", which I remembered was important. Went north through Project Corridor to a dead-end SanFac F.

From here on, Floyd often announced himself as if arriving ("Floyd bounds into the room. 'Floyd here now!'") on turns when he was already with me and just following (343, 344). Another time he "rushes into the room and barrels into you" while he was already beside me. It reads like he keeps leaving and coming back.

#### Turn 349, Repair Room
Achilles the dead robot, and Floyd's lovely eulogy for him. Small doorway north, too small for me. "Ask Floyd to go north" and he came back with a story about a rubber ball: "Just a shiny fromitz board." Then he wandered off, and I waited three turns. When he came back, a new option appeared: "158 ask to get the board". Very clean. He tossed it to me.

#### Turn 358, Planetary Defense again
I still had no idea which board was bad. Shocks from the first, fourth and third. The second slid out, and only then (and in the "40 put in…" list) was it named "fried seventeen-centimeter fromitz board". I found the dead board purely by elimination. Put the shiny one in: "The card clicks neatly into the socket" (a board, not a card). +6 (54).

#### Turn 374, Bio Lock
Floyd asked "Is this...is this a squash court?" when I entered Bio Lock West and again, word for word, one room later in Bio Lock East. Examining the window: "I see nothing special about the window." In the same breath Floyd peered through it and described the danger and the card. The window should show me the lab.

Once Floyd noticed it, the miniaturization card appeared "in the room" with examine/read even though it's on the other side of a closed door. Did Floyd's plan: open, close, wait (three knocks and tearing metal), open, close. The death scene and the ballad are intact and still hit hard 40 years later. +2. Examining the mangled robot: "a tremendous sense of loss overcomes you". Took the card: +1 (57).

#### Turn 389, into the computer
Slid the card (164) and typed 384 (35 384). Station 384, the strip (+4), then north to the relay. It's red plastic, so I set the laser to 1 (116 1), hoping that's red. Examining the speck: "nothing special", although "look inside" had just described it as a blue boulder. Shooting took seven shots of misses and "sizzles" before it vaporized: +2 on the first shot, then +8 (71). The laser got warmer each time. The "shoot something" entry vanished afterwards, and my extra click got the error message 'Option 134 ("shoot something (laser) (laser)")' with the object doubled.

Headed back, and the microbe landed in front of me. Threw the hot laser off the strip (167) and the microbe dived after it. Then: "Main Booth has malfunctioned... switching to Auxiliary Booth": +4 (75). The game also reminded me I'm hungry and that the ration is back in the Library Lobby. Useful, though I never needed it.

#### Turn 412, Lab Office
"The only way out is through the mutant-infested Bio Lab." Gas mask in the desk. Wore it, pushed the red "Eemurjensee Sistum" button (the hissing is a nice cue), saved and opened the door. Mist, stunned mutants, and the PA announced "Cryo-chamber access from Project Control Office now open." The man-eating plant was listed under "In the room" while the other three mutants were under "Characters".

#### Turn 419, the chase
Opened the lab door, then west, west (the bio-lock door had closed again, so I opened it), Main Lab, SW Computer Room, W ProjCon Office. The mural had slid away to show the elevator, but the mural was still offered as scenery. South into the Cryo-Elevator, pushed the button, and the door closed just in time: +5 (80). "Wait for the elevator" bundled the three waits, which was good.

#### Turn 431, Cryo-Anteroom
Veldina, the Flathead, Blather as my toilet attendant, and Floyd repaired: "Maybe we can use them in the sequel..." 80 of 80, Galactic Overlord. Wonderful.

I never needed the radiation lab, the teleport booths, the conference-room combination 982 or Project Corridor East's south door. The endgame came so smoothly that I didn't miss them, but a first-timer might wonder what they were for.
