### Report

- **How it ended:** won, at turn 544 on Day 2 (night), in the Cryo-Anteroom. The planet was revived, but I was stranded because the meteor defenses shot down the rescue ship.
- **Score:** 68 of 80, rank System Captain.
- **Commands:** about 365 in leg 1 and about 317 in leg 2, so about 680 in all, counting saves, restores and refused commands. Six deaths across the game (three in leg 1, three to the microbe in leg 2).
- **Biggest blockers:** (1) The microbe on the strip (#34). You get two actions, and the clue that it's attracted to the laser's heat appears only after you've shot it twice with a non-red setting. It cost me three deaths. (2) The kitchen card soft-lock in leg 1 (#19). (3) The carrying limit, all game (#13, #31): items spilling out of my arms at every pick-up. (4) The ending fires as soon as you leave the Cryo-Elevator, with no warning that unfinished systems (Planetary Defense, Communications) decide how good the ending is (#36).
- **Best moments:** Floyd's death in the Bio Lock, with the Ballad of the Starcrossed Miner (#32), set up perfectly by his finding Lazarus's breast plate in the Infirmary (#25). The shuttle-driving puzzle with its countdown signs (#24). The escape from the mutants to the Cryo-Elevator (#35). The teleport booths turning a three-hour walk into two turns (#33). Reading the computer printout and the library terminal to understand what went wrong on Resida.

### Verdict

Yes. Typing let me play the whole game from the Feinstein to the cryo-chambers: the parser understood nearly everything I tried, and the story, puzzles and Floyd came through intact. The friction came from the game itself (the carrying limit, one-shot timing windows, the abrupt ending), not from typing, and every stuck moment was one I could get out of with SAVE and RESTORE.
