## Playtest diary: click-15, Lawanda to the end

This is round 15 (click-only, from the Lawanda checkpoint to the end).

### Diary

**Before turn 1.** Starting the session from the Lawanda checkpoint. Plan: read the briefing, then look around.

**Turns 260-279 (about 18 commands).** The briefing was clear: I'm feverish, Floyd is with me, I carry a bedistor, pliers and an unfired laser. I climbed the dead escalator to a Fork, went NE to Systems Corridor West and NW into an Infirmary. There: a red spool ("Simptumz uv Xe Dizeez"), a medicine bottle ("Dizeez supreshun medisin") and a sealed emergency ration. "Take all" dropped my shuttle card and couldn't fit the ration; the hint about the uniform pocket was helpful, and the pocket's "put in…" list only offering small things was a nice touch. I pocketed both cards, opened the bottle and "ate" the medicine: fever broke. Dropped the bottle, took the ration. Floyd found Lazarus's breast plate and ran off sobbing - lovely moment.

Plan next: explore Systems Corridor east and the stairway down, find a reader for the spool.

**Turns 280-303.** Systems Corridor has Planetary Defense (blinking "Surkit Boord Faalyur", an access panel with four identical-looking seventeen-centimeter fromitz boards; examining each told me nothing about which one is bad) and Course Control ("Bedistur Faalyur!", a metal cube with a fused bedistor). "Take" said it was fused; then a "take with pliers" option appeared and worked; I put the good bedistor in: +6, score 48. South is the Library Lobby with a green spool (helicopter manual) in the dust and a terminal; "type a number" is a clean way to drive the menus. The project page mentions mass cryogenic freezing of the population.

Plan: west/up into the library proper, the booth to the east, then south. I still need a fromitz board to swap and a spool reader.

**Turns 304-326.** Library has a microfilm reader; the red spool says the disease kills within eight to ten days of first symptoms and the second symptom is needing much more sleep. The fever came back around turn 310 - the medicine only bought an hour or so. Booth 3 (east of the lobby) is a teleport booth with a card slot and buttons 1 and 2. South: Project Corridor East, then the Main Lab (radiation-lock and bio-lock doors, both closed). Lab Storage off the Main Lab gave a lab uniform with a teleportation access card and a paper ("Kombinaashun tuu Konfurins Ruum: 982"), plus a fresh laser battery. The uniform pocket said "There's no room" for the teleport card (three cards already in it). The Computer Room has a red light and a printout: "Malfunkshun in Sekshun 384! Sumuneeng reepaar roobot." ProjCon Office (mural, logo), Project Corridor, Project Corridor West, SanFac F.

Plan: try the teleport booth with the card, look at the booth south of the Computer Room, and find the conference room.

**Turns 327-342.** In SanFac F I shed weight: dropped the pliers, the empty canteen and the shuttle card, swapped the laser's old battery for the new one, and pocketed the teleport card. Then down the stairs north of Systems Corridor West to the Repair Room: Achilles the robot lies dead at the foot of the stairs, and a robot-sized doorway leads north. I asked Floyd to go north; he played with a ball and mentioned "just a shiny fromitz board". After that, a new "ask to get the board" option appeared under Floyd, and he fetched it. Great interplay, though the option only showed in the reprinted list, not in my trimmed view of the turn.

Plan: take the shiny board to Planetary Defense and swap it for the bad one.

**Turns 343-363.** At Planetary Defense I pulled the second board at a guess, and once in hand it was named "fried" in the put-in list. The shiny board clicked in: +6, score 54. Then Booth 3: the teleport card shows "slide through slot" (taken out of my pocket automatically), light says "Redee", brown button took me to Booth 1 by the Conference Room (south door locked from the other side), beige took me to Booth 2 by the Kalamontee Elevator Lobby, tan brought me back to Booth 3. So the teleport net is a shortcut home to my survival kit if I need food.

Plan: the booth south of the Computer Room, then the Main Lab's radiation-lock and bio-lock doors. I'm feverish again; I should watch sleep and hunger.

**Turns 364-384, and a death.** South of the Computer Room is the Miniaturization Booth (slot, numeric keyboard); Floyd asked if we'd "teleport into the computer like Achilles". I need a card for it. Then the radiation lock: I saved in Radiation Lock East. Opening the lab door was refused until the west door was closed - but "close radiation-lock door" was only on offer from the west half, so I walked back. The Radiation Lab (no suit!) had a brown spool (repair-robot instructions) and a powerful portable lamp; the Bio Lab's sinister forms are visible through a crack. Within three turns of leaving I felt sick, vomited, lost my hair (Floyd laughed), and died of radiation poisoning at turn 384. The warning sign was fair, the death quick. Restored to Radiation Lock East, turn 373.

Plan: leave the radiation lab alone and try the bio-lock instead.

**Turns 376-387.** Through the bio-lock (this time "close bio-lock door" was on offer in the west half, so the lock was smooth). At Bio Lock East I examined the window and Floyd offered to fetch the miniaturization card himself: open, he rushes in, close, wait for his knock, open, close. The options followed the plan exactly. He came back clutching the card and died in my lap while I sang the Ballad of the Starcrossed Miner. +2, score 56. Genuinely moving, and the click interface didn't get in the way at all. Took the miniaturization card.

Plan: Miniaturization Booth, slide the card, type a section number - the printout said "Sekshun 384".

**Turns 388-406.** Card +1 (57). Booth: slid the card, typed 384, shrank to Station 384. East to the strip (+4, 61), north twice to Strip Near Relay: a micro-relay in red plastic with a blue speck jamming the contact. Red plastic made me think a red beam passes through, so I saved and set the laser dial from 5 to 1 (the "set to a number" value entry worked nicely). "Shoot something…" listed the speck. First shot: +2 (63) but "just misses"; two more near misses and the laser is getting warm.

Plan: keep shooting; if it overheats, rethink. Watch for whatever lives in here.

**Turns 407-418.** A hunger warning arrived with a helpful nudge ("The emergency ration you are carrying would take care of both"), so I ate the ration: hunger and thirst fixed. It took nine shots to vaporize the speck (hit, then a second hit): +8, score 71, and "Sector 384 will activate". Heading south, a giant microbe dropped onto the strip. The laser was "very warm"; I saved, then chose the laser's "throw off the strip" and the microbe dived after it. Walking back west to the station, the main booth had "malfunctioned" and I landed in an Auxiliary Booth: +4, score 75.

Plan: step out and see what the fixed computer has woken up. I suspect the mutants.

**Turns 418-438, the end.** Saved in the Auxiliary Booth. North is the Lab Office: the only way out is through the Bio Lab. The desk held a gas mask (worn), and the red "Eemurjensee Sistum" button filled the Bio Lab with mist that stunned the mutants. The announcement said the cryo-chamber is open from the Project Control Office. I ran: Bio Lab, lab door, Bio Lock East (past Floyd's body), Bio Lock West (had to open the west door with the mutants "almost upon" me), Main Lab, Computer Room, ProjCon Office - the mural had slid away to a cryo-elevator. Pushed its button as the monsters arrived: +5, score 80. "Wait for the elevator" took me down to the Cryo-Anteroom, where Veldina woke, the Flathead's crew arrived, I was promoted, Blather became my toilet attendant, and Floyd came back repaired with sequel gifts. **Won: 80 of 80, Galactic Overlord, turn 438.**

### Report

- **How far:** to the end. I won at turn 438 (started at turn 260) with **80 of 80** points (from 42), rank Galactic Overlord, Day 2.
- **Commands:** about 195 playtest calls, including menu opens and option reprints. That's well under the 450 cap.
- **Deaths:** one, radiation poisoning after entering the Radiation Lab without a suit (turn 384). Restoring from the single save slot worked.
- **Biggest blockers:** none were hard. The small frictions were:
  - Options that only appear after a failure or a hint: "take with pliers" only after a plain "take" failed, and "ask to get the board" only after Floyd mentioned it.
  - Four identical fromitz boards, with the fried one only named once you've pulled it.
  - The radiation lock's door-close option is only offered from one half of the lock.
  - Nine dice-roll shots at the speck.
  - A few "…" lists missing actions ("eat" for the medicine) or showing a dead one ("close" on the laser).
- **Best moments:** Floyd's sacrifice at the bio-lock, where every step was an option and the ballad landed, and his return at the end. Fetching the shiny board through the robot-sized door. The ration nudge in the hunger warning. "Throw off the strip" for the microbe. The mutant chase to the cryo-elevator, where the options kept pace with the danger.

### Verdict

Yes. The point-and-click interface carried me from the Lawanda platform to the winning ending with no typed commands. Context-sensitive options (slide through slot, shoot speck, throw off the strip, ask Floyd to get the board) surfaced the right verbs at the right moments. The main rough edges are options that only appear after a failed attempt, and a few inconsistent "…" action lists. Neither ever stopped play.
