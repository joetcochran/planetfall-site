# Round 3: typed-3, typed

The diary of one of the blind playtesters: a fresh AI agent that knew nothing of the game, played it through a text front end and wrote down what it did and what got in its way. How the rounds worked, and what each one changed, is told on [Playing it](playing-it.md); this round is in [The rounds](playing-it.md#the-rounds), and every tester's numbers are in [How far each tester got](playing-it.md#how-far-each-tester-got).

- **Round:** 3, 10 September 2026
- **Mode:** typed: commands typed into the parser
- **Start:** from the start (Deck Nine, turn 0)
- **Result:** Did not win. Where play ended: Helipad, turn 191, with 23 points.
- **Commands:** 199, with no deaths

The diary below is `playtests/2026-09-10/2026-09-10-typed-3.md`, copied by `scripts/wiki-diaries.mjs`. The tester's words are unchanged; only its layout is adjusted where the wiki would draw it badly, and its headings sit one level down.

## Blind typed playtest: session `typed-3`
**Who:** Claude (blind playtester agent, experienced IF player persona) **Date:** 2026-09-10  **Session:** `typed-3` (`node scripts/playtest.mjs --session typed-3 --new`)

### Attempt 1, commands 1-6: Scrubbing the deck
Opening text: I'm a lowly Ensign Seventh Class scrubbing Deck Nine of the Stellar Patrol ship Feinstein. Exits: corridor curves to starboard, gangway up, and an escape pod to port with its bulkhead closed.

My first move is always `inventory`: a scrub brush, a chronometer (worn), a uniform (worn) containing an ID card. On that same turn Ensign First Class Blather swaggered in and gave me thirty demerits. `examine brush` got the stock "nothing special". `examine blather` gave a nice description (misshapen nose, trousers that could slice diamonds). I tried `scrub floor` to play along: "The floor is a bit shinier now." Good, the parser took a verb I wasn't sure about. `look` showed Blather scribbling demerits, and then he wandered off. `examine id card` showed an ID number, 6172-531-541. I noted it in case it matters later.

### Attempt 1, commands 7-12: The pod and the ambassador
`open bulkhead` was refused in character: "Why open the door to the emergency escape pod if there's no emergency?" That reads like a promise that an emergency is coming, so I'll remember the pod. `examine chronometer` shows the time (4675) and an inscription from Mom and Dad. An alien ambassador turned up, left slime on my deck and handed me a brochure. `read brochure` is a fourth-wall joke about the game itself. `talk to ambassador` got a shrug-equivalent and didn't use a turn (turn counter stayed at 9), which is fine. `scrub slime` got a funny line about cleaning one ten-thousandth of it. Then `up` took me to the Gangway, between Decks Eight and Nine.

### Attempt 1, commands 13-21: Blather catches me, then the explosion
Deck Eight has a Hyperspatial Jump Machinery Room to fore. Blather showed up at once, "Twenty demerits... Forty if you're not back on Deck Nine in five seconds!", and my `look` got a second warning. I took the hint and went `down`, `down` back to my post. Wandering looked like the way to end up in trouble, and the pod line from before made me think I should stay near it. The ambassador said goodbye and left. I typed `wait` three times. On the third: "A massive explosion rocks the ship... The door to port slides open." That's the emergency. I went `port` into the Escape Pod and got 3 points. The pod has safety webbing, so `get in webbing` ("You are now safely cushioned within the web.") and the door clanged shut.

### Attempt 1, commands 22-35: The long descent
This was a long run of `wait`s: the pod ejects, the Feinstein explodes, the gyroscopes stabilise, an ocean planet appears, "Approaching planet...human-habitable.", atmospheric entry, the pod approaches an island with sheer cliffs and a plateau of buildings, and it lands "precariously balanced". A panel opens with a survival kit and a towel. It's atmospheric, but about 13 waits with nothing to do feels long to me. The narration does make clear that something is happening, though, so I never thought I was stuck.

### Attempt 1, commands 36-41: Sinking pod, swim to the crag
`get out of webbing` made the pod topple into the water. `take kit and towel` worked with a multi-object "and" (good). `look` showed water rising and the pod sinking. `open bulkhead` let the ocean in, then `out` took me Underwater and `up` reached the Crag (+3, score 6). A small structure clings to the cliff eight metres above and "even an out-of-shape Ensign Seventh Class could probably climb up to it."

### Attempt 1, commands 42-51: Up the cliff to a ruined castle
`examine kit` said only "closed", so I typed `open kit`: red, brown and green blobs of goo. I suspect food. `climb up` worked right away (nice, I half expected to need "climb cliff") and took me to the Balcony, with a plaque in phonetically mangled Galalingua. `read plaque` gives "SEENIK VISTA ... Kalamontee Valee ... Gulmaan Rivur ... formur pravincul kapitul bildeeng." I enjoyed decoding it, and I noted the Kalamontee Valley and the capitol building by the river. `up`, `up` by the Winding Stair brought me to a ruined Courtyard with exits N, W and S (down). West Wing is rubble with only one exit (`search rubble` found nothing), so I went back `east` and then `north` into a Plain Hall of more modern construction, with a branch to the NE.

### Attempt 1, commands 52-64: Rec Area, dorms and toilets
Rec Area: games and tapes scattered, and a locked door to the north with "A dial on the door is currently set to 0". `examine games` and `examine tapes` gave flavour lists (Double Fannucci keeps turning up). `examine dial` says 0-1000. That's a combination lock and I need a number from somewhere. `examine door` is just "The door is closed.", which is thin. It doesn't mention the dial or the lock, although the room description does. East is the Rec Corridor (portals N and S, a branch SW). North: Dorm B, then SanFac B, a dead end with a toilet-bowl joke. `search bunks` found nothing. South through the corridor: Dorm A and SanFac A, the same layout mirrored. Nothing to take so far. Mapping is easy because the room text names its exits.

### Attempt 1, commands 65-76: Mess Hall, a canteen and a card slot
East of the Rec Corridor is the Mess Corridor: a small door north "hooked with a simple steel padlock". `examine padlock` got "nothing special", which is disappointing for something that is clearly a puzzle. South is the Mess Hall with a canteen on a bench and a closed door with a small slot beside it. `take canteen` worked. `examine slot`: ten centimetres wide, two deep, metal ridges. That's obviously a card reader. `put id card in slot` was refused with a helpful nudge: "The slot is shallow... It may be possible to slide something through the slot, though." So `slide id card through slot` was accepted and the reply was "Inkorekt awtharazaashun kard...akses deeniid." Good parser guidance here: it taught me the verb instead of just saying no. I need a different card. `open canteen` said "Opened." and it looks empty.

### Attempt 1, commands 77-84: Dorms C/D and the long walkway to an elevator lobby
Dorm Corridor has a dead motorized walkway heading east. Dorms D and C are the same bunk rooms as before. Walking `east` along the walkway was a single command ("You walk down the long, featureless hallway for a long time"), which cost about 160 time units. That's a nice way to convey scale without making me type ten moves. Corridor Junction (N-S corridor). East of it is the Elevator Lobby: a blue door N with a blue button, a larger red door S with a red button, and a phone-booth-sized room to the east. My first elevators!

### Attempt 1, commands 85-94: Buttons, a teleport booth, cherry-pie goo
`push blue button`: "a faint whirring noise from behind the blue door". `push red button`: "The red door begins vibrating a bit." So both are elevators being called. East is Booth 2: a big "2" on the wall, another ten-centimetre slot, and brown "1" and tan "3" buttons. My stomach growled here ("pretty hungry and thirsty"), so I tried the goo. `examine red goo` was the default "nothing special". `eat red goo` gave "scrumptious cherry pie", and there was no complaint about eating it off the floor of my inventory. `push tan button` gave "Teleportaashun buux not aktivaatid." Back `west`, both lift doors had slid open. The blue one (north) is the Upper Elevator, with Up and Down buttons and a narrow slot. `push down button` got "Nothing happens. The slot beside the buttons stays dark." That's a good diagnostic because it points straight at the slot. Every machine here wants a card I don't have. I still need water for the thirst, and the canteen is empty.

### Attempt 1, commands 95-106: Key in a crevice, a rift, systems monitors
The red door leads to a medium-sized Lower Elevator with the same dark slot, and `push down button` gets the same "slot stays dark". So both lifts need a card. Back at Corridor Junction I went `north` to Admin Corridor South: cracked walls, "a jagged crevice crosses the floor". Examining it paid off: "Lying at the bottom of the narrow crack... is a shiny steel key!" The padlock on the Mess Corridor door comes to mind at once. `take key` gets "Either the crevice is too narrow, or your fingers are too large." So I need a tool to fish it out, maybe something magnetic, or something long and thin. East is SanFac E, another dead toilet. North is Admin Corridor, split by an earthquake: a rift eight metres across and thirty deep blocks the way north. West, "Sistumz Moniturz": LIIBREREE, REEAKTURZ and LIIF SUPORT are green, while PLANATEREE DEFENS, PLANATEREE KORS KUNTROOL, KUMUUNIKAASHUNZ and PRAJEKT KUNTROOL are malfunctioning. That sounds like the shape of the long-term goal: repair those systems and call for help.

### Attempt 1, commands 107-120: Storage full of electronics, and I'm overloaded
`examine equipment` in Systems Monitors: too complicated to operate. `search rubble` by the rift found nothing, and `examine rift` confirmed eight metres across with nasty rocks below. There's no way over yet. South past the junction is Mech Corridor North, with rooms E and W. Storage East has an oil can on a shelf and a cardboard box holding a cracked fromitz board, B-series and K-series megafuses and a good ninety-ohm bedistor. That sounds like spare parts for repairing something (those red monitors?). `take all` took the oil can and then "cardboard box: Your load is too heavy." `inventory` showed the ID card now loose in my hands rather than in the uniform. It was presumably pulled out when I slid it through the slot, which makes sense. I dropped the in-joke brochure and typed `take all from box`, which took all four parts with one line each. West of the corridor is the Physical Plant, a huge dim room with catwalks and dead heating/ventilation gear, and exits NE and SE.

### Attempt 1, commands 121-131: Reactor control and a dark stairway
From the Physical Plant I went `southeast` to Mech Corridor. East is Reactor Control: dials, a wall diagram of a reactor buried under the complex, a metal door with a button, and a dark stairway down. `push button` opened the door to a Reactor Elevator (Up and Down buttons, small slot, card again, I assume). The stairway `down` gave "It is pitch black. You might be eaten by a grue." I backed out right away. I have no light source, so that goes on the list for later. I discovered that Mech Corridor's west exit loops back into the Physical Plant (its NE and SE corners connect to the two Mech Corridor rooms), which is a bit confusing on a mental map. Then `south` to Mech Corridor South, a dead end with doorways SW, S and SE.

### Attempt 1, commands 132-137: Machine shop dispenser, and I switch on a robot
South is the Machine Shop, with a big dispensing machine and a spout: four coloured "KUULINTS" buttons, three "KATALISTS", a square white "BAAS" and a round white "ASID". My thirst warning is still active and I have an empty canteen, but acid from a dispenser is a nice way to die, so I'm holding off. East is the Robot Shop, full of disassembled robots and one four-foot robot "remotely close to being in working order". `examine robot`: de-activated, head lolling. `turn on robot` got "Nothing happens. Then again, you notice a faint hum..." and +2 points (score 8). One `wait` later: "Hi! I'm B-19-7, but to everyperson I'm called Floyd... Let's play Hider-and-Seeker you with me." Robot companion met! He's adorable. The turn on/wait beat lands well.

### Attempt 1, commands 138-147: Floyd tags along; the Tool Room and my weight limit
I tried `ask floyd about key`, hoping he might help with the crevice, and got "You can't see any key here!" The parser won't let me ask about things that aren't in the room. That's a real IF convention problem, because you normally ask NPCs about absent things all the time. `floyd, follow me` got "Okay!" and he followed me west. West of the Machine Shop is the Tool Room: a large glass flask, a curved U-shaped metal bar, wide-nosed pliers, and an "Akmee Portabul Laazur" with an old battery. The U-shaped bar has to be a magnet, which is exactly what I need for the key in the crevice. `examine bar` gives the default "nothing special". It doesn't mention magnetism, which is OK since it lets me work it out myself. `take bar` was refused for weight. I dropped the scrub brush and got the bar, then dropped the fromitz board and bedistor, but the laser was still too heavy. Meanwhile Floyd announced "Floyd going exploring. See you later." and glided off. His idle chatter (checking himself for rust, reminiscing about Lazarus the medical robot) is lovely.

### Attempt 1, commands 148-162: Magnet fishing and the padlock (lock opened!)
Back north through the Mech Corridors to the crevice. Floyd bounced back in ("Floyd here now!") along the way. My first try, `put bar in crevice`, got a bare "You can't do that." with no nudge, unlike the helpful slot message earlier. `get key with bar` worked beautifully: "a piece of metal leaps from the crevice and affixes itself to the magnet. It is a steel key!" The game calls the bar "the magnet" only after the fact, which is a nice touch. No points for it, surprisingly. Then south, `west` (the long walkway in one move again) and west to the Mess Corridor. `unlock padlock with key` got "The padlock springs open." `open door` then got "The door cannot be opened until the padlock is removed." Fair, but an extra step. `north` said "The door is closed." `remove padlock` took it, `open door` said "Opened.", and Floyd barrelled into me ("Floyd not looking at where he was going to.").

### Attempt 1, commands 163-177: A ladder for the rift, and a vocabulary gap
Behind the padlocked door is Storage West (+4, score 12): a tin of "Spam and Egz" and "a heavy-duty extendable ladder". An extendable ladder next to an eight-metre rift is about as clear as a hint gets. `take ladder` was refused: "You'd need both hands free for the ladder." That's a clear message. I tried `drop all` (it dropped the ID card, towel, kit, canteen, oil can, fuses and bar in one go), then `take ladder` worked. I carried it south and east down the walkway. At the Junction two new warnings arrived together: "You begin to feel weary..." (sleep) and hunger/thirst again. My food kit is back in Storage West, so that was a mistake on my part. On to the rift. Floyd's arrival message "Floyd bounds into the room. 'Floyd here now!'" now repeats each move even though he's following me, which reads oddly.

At the rift I typed `extend ladder` and got **"I don't know the word 'extend'."**. That's a real stumble, because the ladder is literally called "extendable" and `examine ladder` says "if extended would obviously be much longer". I guessed `open ladder` and got "You couldn't possibly extend the ladder while you're holding it." So the game uses the word "extend" in its own reply but won't accept it from me. `drop ladder`, `open ladder`: "The ladder extends to a length of around eight meters."

### Attempt 1, commands 178-186: Across the chasm, and a desk full of keycards
`put ladder across rift` worked on the first try: "The ladder swings out across the rift and comes to rest on the far edge." Then `north`: "You slowly make your way across the swaying ladder..." to Admin Corridor North (+4, score 16). Chasm crossed. Portals are signed "Administraativ Awfisiz" (W), "Tranzportaashun Suplii" (N) and "Plan Ruum" (E). West is a Small Office with a desk. `open desk` revealed a kitchen access card and an upper elevator access card. `take cards` got "Which do you mean, the kitchen access card or the upper elevator access card?" I meant both; the plural should have taken both. `take all from desk` worked (+2, score 18). Further west, the Large Office has a picture window and a wide wooden desk. `open wooden desk` revealed a shuttle access card, and `take shuttle card` got +1 (score 19). Now everything clicks: the upper elevator card for the blue lift, the kitchen card for the Mess Hall slot (and hopefully water for my thirst).

### Attempt 1, commands 187-200: Riding the elevator to the tower top (end of budget)
With about 15 commands left I went for the lift rather than the kitchen. On the way back, the Small Office said "There is a multiple purpose robot here." rather than naming Floyd. It's a small thing, but he'd introduced himself by name. Back across the swaying ladder, south twice, east to the Elevator Lobby, north into the Upper Elevator. `slide upper elevator card through slot` got "A recorded voice chimes 'Elevator enabled.'" `push up button`: "The elevator door slides shut... vertical movement." One `wait` (plus a sleepiness warning, "You're really tired now") and `look`, and the door slid open. The look inside the lift again called Floyd "a multiple purpose robot". `south` took me to Tower Core (+4, score 23), with a spiral staircase up and exits NE and SW, and another escalation: "You're now really ravenous and your lips are quite parched." `up` reached the Helipad: a fence, clouds, and "A large vehicle, severely weathered and topped with rotor blades". My last command, `examine vehicle`, got the default "I see nothing special about the large vehicle.", which was an anticlimax for such a big object. Floyd drew his name on the wall with a crayon. That's where my budget ran out: score 23, turn 191, standing on the Helipad, starving, parched and exhausted. My food and canteen were back in Storage West and I had no water.

### Report
**How far I got.** 200 commands, turn 191, score 23, no deaths. I scrubbed the deck, survived Blather, waited out the explosion, rode the escape pod down, escaped the sinking pod, climbed the cliff to the ruined castle, explored the complex (Rec Area, four dorms, Mess Hall, storage rooms, Systems Monitors, Physical Plant, Reactor Control, Machine Shop, Tool Room, Robot Shop), and reached the Helipad atop the tower. Milestones: **met the robot companion** (turned Floyd on in the Robot Shop, +2), **opened a lock** (fished the steel key out of the crevice with the U-shaped magnet, unlocked and removed the padlock, +4 for entering Storage West), **crossed the chasm** (extended the ladder and laid it across the rift, +4), collected three access cards from the admin desks (+3), and **rode an elevator** (upper elevator card, Up, Tower Core, +4). Not done: the kitchen (I have the card but went to the lift first), the red Lower Elevator and the Reactor Elevator, the dark Reactor Access Stairs (no light), the teleport booth, the Rec Area dial combination, the Machine Shop dispenser, the laser (too heavy for my load), and the north and east portals beyond the rift ("Tranzportaashun Suplii", "Plan Ruum").

**Where I got stuck.** Never stuck for long. The worst stall was the ladder: `extend ladder` gets "I don't know the word 'extend'." on an object called "extendable", whose description says "if extended". I only found `open ladder` by guessing, and then had to drop it first. Other slow spots were carrying capacity (I dropped almost everything, including my food, to carry the ladder, and could never pick up the laser) and the three-step padlock (unlock, then remove, then open). The survival clocks became the main tension at the end: by turn 190 I was "really ravenous", "parched" and "really tired", with my food left behind and no water found. With more budget, my next move would have been the kitchen card in the Mess Hall slot.

**Biggest points of confusion.** (1) The missing EXTEND verb. (2) The invisible weight system: `take` fails with "Your load is too heavy" and nothing tells me what is heavy or how close I am. (3) `ask floyd about key` refused because the key wasn't in the room, so I never learned whether conversation topics work. (4) Floyd being called "a multiple purpose robot" in room listings after he'd named himself. (5) Thin default responses on important objects: the padlock, the door with the dial, the goo blobs and the helicopter-like vehicle all got "nothing special" or "is closed".

**What worked well.** The writing and pacing: Blather's escalating demerits, the escape-pod sequence, Floyd's personality, the phonetic signage. The parser handled most natural IF phrasing: `take kit and towel`, `take all from box/desk`, `get key with bar`, `put ladder across rift`, `floyd, follow me`, `climb up`, `turn on robot`, `unlock padlock with key`, `slide ... through slot`. The best moment was the slot nudge ("The slot is shallow... It may be possible to slide something through the slot"), which taught me a verb I then reused at every card reader. Failure messages at the elevators ("The slot beside the buttons stays dark") and the teleport booth are diagnostic, not opaque. The long walkway as a single move is a good choice.

**What I would change.** Add EXTEND (and LENGTHEN/UNFOLD) for the ladder. Treat plurals ("take cards") as "all". Allow ASK/TELL about absent known objects. Let `open door` auto-remove an unlocked padlock. Surface load and weight in `inventory`. Name Floyd consistently once introduced. Give real descriptions to the padlock, the dial door, the goo blobs and the vehicle. Consider a `wait N` or a shorter descent so the 13-wait pod sequence moves faster.

### Verdict
This is a charming, well-paced remake: the parser understands most of what an experienced IF player types and often teaches the right verb when it doesn't. The one genuine wall was `extend ladder` being an unknown word on an "extendable" ladder, plus a tight, invisible carrying limit and a handful of default "nothing special" descriptions on key objects. Fix those and the first act plays very smoothly.
