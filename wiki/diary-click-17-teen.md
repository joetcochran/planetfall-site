### Report

**Where I got to:** the Kitchen in Kalamontee (life 2), with the shuttle, kitchen and upper elevator cards and a full stomach. I never reached Lawanda Platform. Life 1 got as far as crossing the rift and finding the Small Office cards, then I died of hunger on the long hallway at turn 318, score 25.

**Turn / score / commands:** life 2 ended at turn 105, score 23 (life 1 died at turn 318 with score 25). About 450 commands in total: roughly 329 in life 1 and 122 in life 2. That's my own tally, so it could be off by a few.

**Moments I'd have quit:**
- Turns ~138-170 (life 1): about 30 turns walking between identical dorms and bathrooms, looking for a shuttle card with no lead.
- Turn 205: I slept until morning because the notice said "service resumes each morning", walked all the way back, and the shuttle was still locked. I'd have gone and looked it up here.
- Turns 208-223: 13 clicks of walking across the whole map to reach one dead-end exit.
- Turn 267: I only examined the crevice out of desperation, after about 100 turns stuck.
- Turn 318: I died of hunger 3 rooms from the kitchen, after finally getting the kitchen card, and had never saved. For real this is where I'd close the game and not come back.
- Restart: the pod flight again, 13 "wait" clicks I couldn't skip.

**Best moments:**
- Throwing the scrub brush at Blather's nose ("ten thousand demerits").
- The Feinstein exploding behind the pod, then swimming up with the depth counter.
- Floyd waking up: "Hi! I'm B-19-7... Let's play Hider-and-Seeker you with me."
- Tickling Floyd so the card falls out ("That was the itchy thing"). I figured that one out myself, the best moment of the game.
- The chain of magnet, key, padlock, ladder and rift. Each step opened the next.
- Floyd reacting to a save: "Are we gonna try something dangerous now?"
- The Blather dream after sleeping, and the alien ambassador with celery on the replay.
- The octagonal canteen fitting the octagonal niche.

**What would have kept a player like me going:**
- A way to know what I'm trying to do. Even one line like "find a way to get the shuttle running" after the shuttle refuses. The Plan Room maps helped a lot, but I only found them after about 300 turns.
- A nudge to save before risky stuff, or an autosave. Losing 300+ commands to hunger with no save is the biggest problem.
- The hunger clock is harsh. Something like "you'll need a real food source soon, the goo won't last" early on would help. So would the long hallway warning me that it takes 3 hours when I'm starving.
- Fast travel to rooms I've already visited. Half my clicks were walking through the same corridors.
- A "skip" or "wait until something happens" button for the opening and the pod flight.
- A hint when I'm stuck on something I walked past, like the crevice. For example, Floyd pointing at it, or the room text making it stand out more.
- The laser giving +2 points on the padlock sent me the wrong way. Points should mean I'm on the right track.

### Verdict
The funny parts are really funny, and Floyd is the best video game robot ever, but I'd have quit for good when I starved to death 300 turns in with no save. If I had a friend who could tell me "save, and the shuttle card is in the big office" I'd come back to see Lawanda, but on my own probably not. It's more walking than playing.
